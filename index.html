AI-Assisted AR-to-Bank Reconciliation Automation

Finance automation prototype built with n8n

A personal finance automation prototype that compares AR Ledger and Bank Remittance records, identifies matched transactions and reconciliation exceptions, validates results against a controlled Answer Key, and generates transaction-level and summary reports.

Business Problem

Manual AR-to-bank reconciliation requires comparing records across separate sources, identifying missing or duplicate transactions, investigating differences, and preparing an exception report. The prototype was designed to reduce repetitive comparison work and standardize exception identification.

How the Automation Works

StageWhat happens
InputAR Ledger + Bank Remittance CSV files
StandardizationNormalize reconciliation key, customer/payer, amount, date, currency and source
ReconciliationMatch records by recon_key and compare amount, currency, customer and date
Exception handlingIdentify AR-only, Bank-only, duplicates and attribute discrepancies
ValidationCompare automated results with a controlled Answer Key
OutputGenerate a 1,096-record reconciliation report and summary CSV

Reconciliation Logic

Records are grouped by reconciliation key. Clean 1-to-1 records are compared across amount, currency, customer/payer, and transaction date. Structural exceptions include AR-only, Bank-only, duplicate AR, duplicate Bank, and duplicate entries in both sources.

Validation Approach

A controlled Answer Key was used to validate the automated classification across defined test scenarios. Individual checks confirmed genuine matches, missing bank transactions, unmatched bank transactions, and discrepancy scenarios. The final validation output retained all 1,096 records.

Important Design Notes

  • Date matching uses an agreed ±1-day tolerance. Transactions outside that tolerance are flagged for investigation.
  • The current prototype is primarily rule-based reconciliation automation. AI is positioned as an extension for exception analysis, prioritization, and investigation support rather than being overstated as the core matching engine.
  • Synthetic/controlled data was used for the prototype and validation.
